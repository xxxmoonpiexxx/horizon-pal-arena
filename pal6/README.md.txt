# Horizon Pal Arena

A side-by-side telemetry mashup connecting Forza Horizon 6 telemetry to Palworld (UE4SS / LogicMods). Turn your driving dynamics, drifts, and collisions into active Pal co-driver elemental attacks!

## Features
- **Co-Driver Mechanics**: Pals sit shotgun, reacting to RPM, speed, and cornering with live elemental spells.
- **Safe Telemetry Bridge**: Reads raw local UDP telemetry without altering encrypted game memory.
